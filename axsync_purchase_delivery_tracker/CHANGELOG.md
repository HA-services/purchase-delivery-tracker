# Changelog

## 18.0.1.0.0 (initial release)

- Delivery dashboard with KPI tiles (purchase orders, pending, partially received, overdue, completed, outstanding quantity, on-time rate, average delay), period selector, drill-down, most overdue orders and supplier ranking.
- Delivery Tracking report per purchase order and Delivery Lines report per purchase order line, with expected date, last receipt date, ordered, received and remaining quantity, delivery status and delay in days.
- Supplier Performance report: on-time percentage, average delay, late deliveries, completed orders and ranking.
- Overdue Delivery Analysis with overdue age buckets.
- Delivery alerts for upcoming, overdue and partially received deliveries, sent as activities to the buyer, once per situation. Daily scheduled action and a Send Alerts Now button.
- Delivery Tracking tab and badge on purchase orders, and an on-time delivery button on supplier records.
- Received quantities come from validated stock receipts through the native received quantity of the purchase line: partial receipts, multiple receipts, returns and unit of measure conversions are supported.
- Multi-company record rules and read-only access for the Purchase User group.
