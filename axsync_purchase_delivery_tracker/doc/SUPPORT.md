# Support

Published by AXSync, www.axsync.com.

## How to get help

1. Email hunain@axsync.com, or use the Contact the Author option on the Odoo Apps Store page of this product.
2. Include: Odoo version and edition (Community or Enterprise), module version, whether Odoo.sh or self-hosted, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot or the server log lines if there is an error.

## What is covered

Defects in the delivered module and questions about how it behaves. Customisation of the reports, other Odoo versions and other applications are separate work.

## Reporting a defect

Describe the steps to reproduce on a database with a small amount of data. A purchase order reference and the report where the figure looks wrong are usually enough to investigate.
